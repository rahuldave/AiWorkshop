# AI tools and prompt engineering

The [PowerPoint draft](output/03-AI-Tools-and-Prompt-Engineering.pptx) has 26 slides. Speaker notes cover a 60 minute session, including eight minutes of practice.

The [practice handout](output/03-Practice.md) contains the document, prompts, checks, and a worked answer. The exercise uses fictional text.

## Session focus

Participants write a request, supply context, and check the result against a document. They then revise one instruction and compare the answers.

The session introduces chat, file input, and web search through the work each feature supports. Account and workspace settings can affect access.

The Credcore case explains retrieval, prompt feedback, and AI judges. The contract comparison and sequence of agent tool calls remain assigned to Deck 4.

## Source use

The deck adapts four slides from [LLMAndPrompt.key](sources/work/LLMAndPrompt.key). It keeps the original pictures and removes the technical detail around them.

The adapted slides show instructions and output format, examples, conversation history, and prompt versions. Original diagram credits remain visible.

The retrieval and judge diagrams come from the [Credcore notes](sources/credcore/Extracting%20Information%20from%20Contracts.md). Those notes credit the diagrams to Chip Huyen.

The [evaluation excerpt](sources/work/evalnotions.pdf) supplies background for clear criteria and the limits of AI judges.

The practice answers are worked examples. The deck labels them separately from the recorded Credcore material.
