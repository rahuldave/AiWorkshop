# AI Training Workshop

## A Journey from Understanding AI to Building Your Own AI Ecosystem

**Time:** 10:00 AM–3:00 PM  
**Audience:** Nontechnical participants  
**Approach:** Everyday examples, discussion, demonstrations, and practical activities

> **Central theme:** Don't just learn to use AI. Learn to shape it, direct it, and build an AI ecosystem that reflects your goals, values, and vision.

## Workshop Agenda

| Time | Session | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **10:00–11:00 AM** | **AI Context: An Introduction & History** | A nontechnical journey from embeddings to language models. Explore how AI represents relationships, uses context, and generates language, with a brief look at the milestones that brought us here. |
| **11:00 AM–12:00 PM** | **AI Agency: Building the AI Ecosystem We Want for Ourselves** | Move from passive use toward intentional choices about AI. Identify the work we want help with, the values we want to preserve, and the decisions we want to keep in human hands. |
| **12:00–1:00 PM** | **Lunch Break** | Connect, recharge, and reflect. |
| **1:00–2:00 PM** | **AI Tools & Prompt Engineering** | Explore practical AI tools, communicate clearly with AI, and develop prompts that produce useful results. Practice checking and improving the output. |
| **2:00–2:30 PM** | **AI Agents: The Name Is Bond** | Discover how agents use AI models and tools to work through a task. Explore what we can delegate, what permissions an agent needs, and where human review belongs. |
| **2:30–3:00 PM** | **Conclusion & Summation: From Learning to Action** | Bring together the day's lessons, reflect on opportunities, and choose concrete next steps for integrating AI into personal and professional life. |

## The Workshop Journey

### 1. UNDERSTAND

**AI Context:** Where AI came from and where we are. Build an intuitive understanding of embeddings and language models through familiar examples.

### 2. IMAGINE

**AI Agency:** What kind of AI ecosystem do we want? Explore how our goals and values should guide the tools, workflows, and boundaries we choose.

### 3. CREATE

**AI Tools & Prompt Engineering:** Make AI work for us. Practice turning an intention into a clear request, then evaluate and improve the result.

### 4. ACTIVATE

**AI Agents:** Put AI to work on our behalf. Learn how to define a task, provide useful context, and retain control over consequential actions.

### 5. INTEGRATE

**Conclusion:** Turn knowledge into action. Leave with one useful experiment and a way to judge whether it helps.
